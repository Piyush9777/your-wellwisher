The STAR Method Framework

Recruiters at Amazon, Google, and Microsoft evaluate behavioral answers against the STAR method to measure real impact and problem-solving maturity.

S - Situation
Set the context, project constraints, and specific challenge faced.
T - Task
Define your personal ownership and what needed to be achieved.
A - Action
Detail step-by-step initiatives YOU implemented to solve the issue.
R - Result
Quantify measurable results (%, latency, time saved, awards).
